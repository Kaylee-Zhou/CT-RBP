Project Background

RNA-binding proteins play a crucial role in gene regulation, RNA processing and disease occurrence. Traditional experimental methods for identifying RNA-protein interactions are time-consuming and labor-intensive. This platform combines deep learning and knowledge graphs, aiming to provide rapid and accurate predictions and offer suggestions for wet-lab validation.
